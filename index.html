      How the tournament worked
      1. In every session, the four troops were ranked by their team Lichess score.
      2. That ranking gave points: 1st = 4, 2nd = 3, 3rd = 2, 4th = 1.
      3. If troops tied in a session, they shared the points (for example 2.5 each).
      4. The Points column decided the standings. Total Lichess score only broke ties.
      5. The top 2 troops after the last session reached the final.
      6. The final was one game on a physical board. The winner's troop became champion.
