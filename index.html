      How scoring works

      1. In every session, the four troops are ranked by their team Lichess score.
      2. That ranking gives points: 1st = 4, 2nd = 3, 3rd = 2, 4th = 1. The Power Hour counts double.
      3. If troops tie in a session, they share the points (for example 2.5 each).
      4. The Points column decides the standings. Total Lichess score only breaks ties.
      5. The top 2 troops after the last session reach the final.
